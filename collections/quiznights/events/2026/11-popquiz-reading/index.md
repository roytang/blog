We attended [PopQuiz: Ang PANGET na Trivia Night](https://www.facebook.com/PopQuizPH/posts/2042993256517575) at a new venue: Pancho Cafe in Kapitolyo.

In attendance: Me, Chry, Jaime, Chery, Kim.

This one was obviously themed around books. I once called our group "illiterates" so I had low expectations, but we did surprisingly well and won the event!

This is only the fourth game of the Pop Quiz X tenth anniversary league that we have attended! It is the first one we have won, but we skipped so many of the games (I think half of them so far) we are around 400pts behind the leading teams so winning the league is not in the cards for us lol.

Round breakdown:

1. YA Covers (quickfire, 2pts each) - 13pts
2. Scifi - 7pts+6 for successful gamble (I, Robot!)
3. Adaptations (casting couch) - 9pts x2 for powerup
4. Fantasy - 6pts
5. Reference - 5pts
6. Romance (quickfire, 2pts each) - 17pts!! We are lucky Chery and Kim were with us!
7. (Hail Mary) Horror/Mystery - 5pts

We didn't even need that Hail Mary to win! The prizes included a Pancho Cafe GC and a bunch of popcorn and luncheon meat tins that we split amongst ourselves.
