I attended a solo quiz night! The trivia team didn't want to go because the venue was a bit far north, but it was close to me, it was Moshi Gran Cafe on Congressional Ave. I decided to go alone since I felt I had a reasonable shot, but also to check out the venue since it was new.

[Trentahin Quiz Night: A Millenial Pop Culture Quiz Nigh](https://www.facebook.com/AboutQuizPH/posts/1035315765787864)

Round by round recap:

1. Movies - 5pts x2 for powerup
2. TV - 2pts
3. Music - 1pt
4. Internet - 1pt
5. People - 4pts
6. Technology and Things - 5pts
7. Pabibo: X-Men vs Street Fighter (12 answers correct out of 17 possible, wager was 10) - 10pts. I forgot so many of the playable characters, and also confused it a bit with Children of the Atom.

I somehow managed to still finish in 3rd place thanks to that Pabibo, which I guess is amazing given I was solo. If I had 1-2 teammates to help me with local movies/TV and music lyrics it was a winnable night.
