We attended [The Magic of Disney Trivia Night](https://www.facebook.com/PopQuizPH/posts/2058610098289224) at Vault GH.

In attendance: Me, Chry, Chery.

I had high hopes for this one, but we did not do so well. All of our rounds were middling.

Round breakdown:

1. Magical characters - 5pts.
2. Magical locations - 6pts.
3. Voice lines and voice actors (quickfire x2) - 11pts.
4. Magical creatures (quickfire x2) - 10pts.
5. Magical items - 5pts x2 for power-up.
6. Actors and actresses - 6pts x2 for gamble. As predicted, this was a casting couch, but it was harder than we expected.
7. (Hail Mary) Backmasked Disney songs (quickfire) - 7pts

We finished a mere 7th place among 12 teams! A whole 30 points away from first place, we had no chance!
