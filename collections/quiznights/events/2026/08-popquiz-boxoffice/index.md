We attended [Box Office: PopQuiz Trivia Night about Movies](https://www.facebook.com/events/1590854315782988/).

In attendance: Me, Chry, Chery, Jaime, Faith.

This is the first game of the Pop Quiz X tenth anniversary league! Unfortunately we did not do as well as I hoped.

Round breakdown:

1. Romance/Drama - 6pts.
2. Horror/Thriller - 4pts. One of our weak spots, so this is better than expected.
3. Musicals - 8pts.
4. Comedy - 4pts x2 for successful gamble. Not the best choice.
5. Action/Adventure - 7pts x2 for power-up.
6. Pinoy Movies - 6pts.

We finished a mere 4th place among 11 teams, which isn't too bad (and the scorer counted more points for us than we did). Hopefully the rest of the league goes better.
