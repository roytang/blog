Anyway, I'm looking for app recommendations for reading ebooks on my iPad.

I have a large collection of ebooks (mostly epubs and PDFs) on my desktop PC (not a Mac) that I can access on my home network via a webserver. I would like to be able to access them via a ereader app that can open the site and download ebook files directly (instead of having to use the iPad's browser).

As an example, for CBR and CBZ files I currently use the Chunky reader app (an app I also heard about from Reddit many years ago!). This app has an in-app web browser that lets me queue up multiple downloads at a time and then they are available locally in the app for me to choose what to read. I would like similar functionality for my ereader app.

I was previously using the Kindle app, but this one is a bit annoying because I have to download the files separately and send to the app and I think that goes through Amazon's servers first or something before being downloaded back to the Kindle app. This is ok for downloading and reading a book at a time, one of my use cases is I also want to be able to browse entire runs of old magazines (mostly from the internet archive) and I would like to download all the issues and just go through them at will.

Features I don't care for and in fact would rather not have:

- social features
- any kind of cloud sync
- any sort of book store
- anything that requires an account really

Of course the reading experience itself should be decent. I would not say no to the ability to highlight segments in an ebook and export them later (the Kindle app has this).

Apps that I have already tried and discarded:

- Apple Books
- ReadEra
- Pocketbook

If it matters, my iPads are a bit old (I have both a 6th gen and a 7th gen one), so anything too demanding performance wise is probably not a good idea.

I will continue to try other apps, but I was hoping the internet might have some good suggestions.
