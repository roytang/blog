### Movies

#### Oppenheimer (2023)

Watched on 2026-07-17: ★★★★★



{{% photos oppenheimer-2023 %}}

 This one started off slow and given it was 3 hours I thought I might need multiple days to finish it, but nope, it was good enough that I got engrossed and finished it in the same day. I once again had some trouble with Nolan's sound mixing, finding myself often having to adjust volume up to hear dialog, then adjust volume down again when special effects and background sounds and explosions and what not came up. Some fantastic performances in there, especially from Murphy and Downey, and an excellent portrayal of the conflict and guilt that might come with creating weapons of mass destruction. I accept that this was a best picture winner.



[Review on Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/hungryroy/film/oppenheimer-2023/)

#### Stargate (1994)

Watched on 2026-07-18: ★★★★



{{% photos stargate-1994 %}}

 I think I saw this one in the cinema back in the day, though I'm not sure I appreciated it as little more than a fun action flick with some sci-fi thrown in (I would have been 16yo!). That is still largely true, but modern sensibilities may point out a bit of a white savior narrative going on in the film. Not super problematic for me, but kind of a tired trope, though I suppose it was a different time. I do like that the denizens of the remote planet didn't magically speak or learn English, though Daniel (James Spader) magically able to almost immediately learn and speak their variant of ancient Egyptian stretches the imagination a bit. I suspect the Ra portrayal would have creeped out 16yo me back in the day. Djimon Honsou is here!



[Review on Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/hungryroy/film/stargate/)

#### Good Luck, Have Fun, Don't Die (2025)

Watched on 2026-07-20: ★★★★★



{{% photos good-luck-have-fun-dont-die-2025 %}}

 I came into this not knowing what to expect, but I ended up liking it a lot. It feels like 3 or 4 Black Mirror tossed together with a through line featuring a manic Sam Rockwell and some fun cross-references across the different back stories, and ending that's either a bit dark or a bit ambiguous.



[Review on Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/hungryroy/film/good-luck-have-fun-dont-die/)

#### The Odyssey (2026)

Watched on 2026-07-22: ★★★★★



{{% photos the-odyssey-2026 %}}

 I was worried about watching this on a regular screen rather than IMax but it was still pretty great and epic. Watching this so soon after Oppenheimer really emphasizes all of the storytelling techniques Nolan loves to use like the constant looping back to key scenes, it's great. 

Despite largely knowing how this story was going to go and how even each segment was going to end up, the film does a great job of ratcheting up the tension along the way. Alternating scenes between the people waiting for odysseus and odysseus himself was a pretty good pacing tool.

Generally happy with the performances. Tom Holland as the very reserved Telemachus was a fine contrast with his Spider-Man persona, and he had great chemistry in his scenes with Jon Bernthal's Menelaus, and I look forward to seeing them together again in Spider-Man



[Review on Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/hungryroy/film/the-odyssey-2026/)

#### Spider-Man: Brand New Day (2026)

Watched on 2026-07-29: ★★★★★



{{% photos spider-man-brand-new-day-2026 %}}

 This was incredibly good; I think best MCU film post-Endgame so far, and even best Tom Holland Spider-Man so far, maybe even rivaling some of the best Infinity Saga films. 

The writing and pacing were excellent and the web-swinging was cool and it was great to see a more grounded Spider-Man focused on street level concerns. They even introduced Jean deWolff! The film made good use of Banner and the chemistry between Peter and Frank was great.

My favorite thing about this is that the marketing and the trailers were very good at hyping up the movie without spoiling major plot points. The movie even had one surprise cameo I was not expecting at all!

There is a pre-credits scene and a post-credits scene, but honestly might not be worth waiting for that last one.



[Review on Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/hungryroy/film/spider-man-brand-new-day/)

#### Mission: Impossible – The Final Reckoning (2025)

Watched on 2026-08-31: ★★★½



{{% photos mission-impossible-the-final-reckoning-2025 %}}

 Act 1: Spend way too much time explaining WTH happened in the previous film.

Act 2: Spend way too much time explaining depressurization. Also, Ethan Hunt spends way too much time alone underwater in a cramped sub.

Act 3: Spend way too much time explaining how they're going to use their McGuffins to trick the magic genie and trap it in a box.

I had low expectations for this one because frankly I was not excited by having a magic AI genie as a doomsday threat, and Gabriel seemed kind of dumb.

Not my favorite MI, but the film is entertaining enough, though I think the stunts were better in the previous film. I do like the callbacks to the earlier films, especially random characters given new expanded roles.

Tom Cruise is kinda showing his age. I kind of hoped that he would somehow be passing the baton for someone to take over this franchise but apparently not.

#### NCIS S1-5 and S21

Watched on 2026-08-24: ★★★★½

Checking [my list of TV reviews](/lists/media/tv/), it turns out I have never written about NCIS, so here we go: I started getting into this show and a few other procedurals during a brief work hiatus in 2010 when these were often shown on cable TV. I think I started it around S5 because I do remember seeing at least a few episodes with Jenny Shepard who left the show at the end of that season. 

I've watched it on and off through the years; it's not really the kind of show you just binge fanatically, more like comfortable TV that's doesn't require much thinking and is good for background noise. The characters and their interactions are reasonably interesting and the cases can be as well. This paragraph is probably applicable to most procedurals that last a reasonable amount of time.

NCIS in particular however is well-known for one thing: [ridiculous tech-related scenes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kl6rsi7BEtk) lol. As a person who works in tech, I am mostly just amused.

Recently the first 5 seasons were added to Prime Video. Because I started watching the series a few seasons in, I took the opportunity to start watching the first few seasons as well. I wanted to watch at least the first 2 seasons because I know Kate Todd (who starts the series from the very first episode of S1) dies at the end of S2. At the start of the series, the field team is actually just Gibbs and Dinozzo! They meet and recruit Kate in the first episode, and McGee doesn't make his first apperance as a recurring character until the second episode, and doesn't officially join the team until season 2 I think? Abby and Ducky are already the backend team, but Ducky has a different assistant at first and Palmer doesn't join until later. When Kate is killed, Ziva joins the team starting in S3, and from there is the status quo I mostly started with. Jenny Shepard joins the cast as NCIS director at some point, and she is killed unceremoniously (off-screen!) at the end of S5. NCIS does **not** have a good track record of treating its women characters well. (Somewhere along these 5 seasons they also killed another recurring female NCIS agent.)

Before these seasons I also watched the very short S24 (only 10 episodes!). It's almost impossible now to track which of the in-between seasons I'd seen, but I do know I've seen all the seasons between when Gibbs (Mark Harmon) left and this one. I mostly watched this one because in this season they kill off Ducky because the actor died. As of this season, the only remaining regular cast members from those first 5 seasons are McGee and Palmer!

Seasons 22 and 23 are also on Prime Video; these are the latest ones, season 24 starts airing in a couple of months. But I think I'll hold off on more NCIS for now because I'm still trying to make my way through another long-running show, but I'm sure I'll get back there eventually.
