When thinking about what to write for these posts, I often start by looking through [the past birthday posts](/blog/tags/birthday/). I wrote [the first one](/2002/08/birthday-24/) in 2002 when I was 24, literally half a lifetime ago!

At the time I was unemployed and looking for a job and spending my time playing too many video games. A new Spider-Man film had just come out. The US had started to become enbroiled in mideast conflicts again (as a result of 9/11). A couple of years earlier the PH president was impeached and the dotcom bubble had just burst. A very familiar environment.

It feels like the more things change, the more they stay the same. As the saying goes "History doesn’t repeat itself but it often rhymes."

I think the difference is that back then I was still young, idealistic, bright-eyed, optimistic, and dreaming big. Today I'm old, worried about health concerns, taking care of my parents, aware of many of the world's ills, my optimism and faith in humanity severely challenged by the past decade and current trends, and all I really want is to be left alone to enjoy my shows and video games without any major crises coming up. Sigh.

I miss that version of me. I don't have it so bad now (a life of privilege etc), and I'm thankful for many blessings over the years. But I think the world is not as good as I would have imagined it in 2002. 

In many ways I still consider myself the same person as I was back then; I don't think I ever really "grew up" despite being a functioning adult. But I want to never lose sight of that youthful optimism that I used to consider a big part of my identity. The world doesn't make that easy, but I just have to keep reminding myself that we choose how we respond to the things happening around us and our place in it and hope that I can still keep that optimism moving forward, even as I may have to find a new place for myself in this world.
