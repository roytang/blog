I've written a bit about [750 Words](https://750words.com/) before, [the last time in 2019](/2019/03/750words/). It's a service I started using for private journalling in 2010 on and off and since 2020 I've been writing there on a daily basis. 

The site has this feature called badges which you earn based on your writing stats. You can see the badges I've earned on [my profile page](https://750words.com/person/hungryroy), which I only now realized is publicly accessible. (The writing itself is not.)

Anyway, one of the badges is called *Phoenix* and is awarded for writing 100 days in a row. Phoenix badge holders have an additional perk: if they like, they can fill out a form interview and submit a picture of their writing environment so they appear in a page called [The Hall of Phoenixes](https://750words.com/hall-of-phoenixes). I submitted mine last week, and I like to have all my online writing (such as it is) available on this site so I am "cross-posting" it here.

[Here is my interview on the Hall of Phoenixes page](https://750words.com/hall-of-phoenixes/hungryroy). The content is copied below, typos and all.

(Sadly, this interview is much less than 750 words!)

---

## [hungryroy](https://750words.com/person/hungryroy) 🌙

Joined the Hall of Phoenixes on Apr 10, 2021. Their longest writing streak to date is 1155 days.

{{< photos >}}

### What do your friends call you?

Roy

### How did you learn about 750words.com?

I started writing on 750words back in 2010, so this story has been lost to time! I may have been following Buster Benson on Twitter maybe and heard it from there? I was already blogging regularly, so adding private journaling seemed like a good idea at the time. I stopped for a long while after 2012 and resumed in Jan 2021 and have kept it going since then!

### What are some of your interests, hobbies, and life experiences?

Gaming, movies, tinkering around with software, blogging since 2002! (Visit my blog on https://roytang.net)

### What kinds of things do you write about, generally?

It's mostly a summary of the previous day and thinking through what's I expect for the day and the near future. Sometimes I also use it for thinking through other issues. I often use the archive to help me remember what happened on particular days and such.

### What helped motivate you to write for 100 days straight?

Honestly I just love maintaining streaks! It's a very easy way to get me hooked on something.

### If you could wave a magic wand and add or change anything about the site, what would you wish for?

No GenAI stuff. I regularly export my writings, but I would love it if the analytics like mood etc could be exported also somehow.

### Anything else you'd like to add as an official inductee into the Hall of Phoenixes?

I love doing this and honestly hope the service goes on forever (if it goes down maybe I'll just make a similar service myself lol) and I hope more people write more both publicly and privately, it's really good for your brain.
