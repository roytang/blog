### My Week

Another rainy week, though it let up a bit the past few days. Google Fit says I walked 76,313 steps from last Sunday to yesterday, breaking well past my quota and making up for my shortfall the previous week. This was mostly to me doing an "errands day" in the middle of the week which involved two malls, two cities, some old haunts, and commuting through rush hour traffic in the rain like the "good" old days.

I considered the past 7 days as the "birthday week" so I indulged myself a bit, ate a lot, gained some weight. Celebrated my birthday with the fam, and celebrated some other people's birthdays too. Met up some friends I hadn't seen in around a decade. Bought a new sling bag; the old one from [the everyday carry post](/2023/11/everyday-carry/) already had damaged zippers and such. Had an entire day to myself at home.

One cold night I was surprised by a large insect suddenly flying around my room. At first I thought it was an unusually large cockroach, but turns out it was a moth! I don't know how it got indoors. Sadly it had to die as I had no way to just catch it and release it outside.

Bought a new used Ipad from a friend, only a generation newer than the one I'm currently using, which already has a bad battery. Ran into some trouble with the activation lock which we were unable to bypass due to some account nonsense. Hopefully Apple CS can resolve it, otherwise I'd have to give it back.
