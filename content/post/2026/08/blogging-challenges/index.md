Around this time I often see some people posting about the [Blaugust](https://indieweb.org/Blaugust) blogging challenge to post every day during August. I will often see those posts and think that maybe I should participate, but I very quickly decide that it's not for me.

I think blogging challenges like Blaugust and [100 Days to Offload](https://100daystooffload.com/) are nice challenges to help people post on their blogs more regularly, especially if they're new to the hobby. And I do love it when people post more so definitely do those challenges if you like!

But for me personally I think I'm past the sort of thing where I require myself to do something on a daily basis, as that can give me a bit of psychic damage when I fail. I'd rather be chill and write at my own pace. Besides, I don't think I actually need additional help trying to blog regularly. I've had more than 100 posts a year since 2018!
