Previously: [Retro Review: Tales of Graces F (PS3!)](/2026/06/tales-of-graces-f/)

Continuing my year of playing Tales games on the very retro console PS3 with *Tales of Xillia*! Tales of Xillia is the thirteenth main title in the [Tales](/tag/talesof/) series of JRPGs and is the the fifth one I played chronologically. It follows dual protagonists Jude Mathis and Milla Maxwell as they fight to determine the fates of humans and spirits in the world of Rieze Maxia.

### Collector's Edition

This game was the first time I ever bought a collector's edition for a game! I purchased it in Aug 2013 I guess as a bit of a birthday gift to myself. 

The main attraction of this edition was the gorgeous Milla figurine, here's a picture from just after the purchase:

### 2013 and Finishing the Game with Milla

I purchased the game on its release date on Aug 6, 2013! I finished the Milla side of the main game on Aug 25, less than 3 weeks later, with around 51 hours of playtime. I did a bit of postgame stuff after that and into the first couple of weeks of September before getting distracted by other things and leaving the game alone for THIRTEEN YEARS!

### 2026 and Completing the Game with Jude

After replying *Tales of Graces F* earlier this year, I proceeded to pick up Tales of Xillia again. Like Graces F, I took so long to come back to this game that a new version has already been released for modern platforms: [Tales of Xillia Remastered](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2246670/Tales_of_Xillia_Remastered/), but I prefered to play my old save from 2013! I started the second run around June, so I played it for roughly two months.

The game makes you choose at the start whether to follow Milla's path (which I already finished) or Jude's path, so it was Jude's turn in New Game+ with the appropriate Grade Shop upgrades. The stories are mostly the same, with some sections varying between the two characters. I didn't save a lot of time for the second run; I still re-watched most of the cutscenes to refresh my memory of the story. I actually forgot about the big plot twist halfway through!

I finished Jude's path *and* managed achievement completion yesterday. Had to follow a guide for the missable side events, but otherwise it was pretty straightforward.

Total playtime: Around 81 hours. [HLTB has Tales of Xillia](https://howlongtobeat.com/game/9610) at 51 hours for main+sides and 87 hours for completionist runs, so I am largely on point.

### Story and Combat and Mechanics

I loved Xillia's story and am glad I got to experience it again, especially since Xillia 2 is still there waiting to be played. They really worked this time to make a "sympathetic" villain and by all accounts there was even an initial plan for them to be playable characters, though that was discarded early.

Graphically it is also a decent leap over Graces F (understandable as that was originally a Wii game), and I really like how a lot of characters are animated.

Mechanically, it is less expansive than Graces F. There's no real item crafting to be done and I didn't need to grind for materials or anything like that. I feel like Graces F's combat was deeper, but Xillia's is still fun, especially with the introduction of linking and linked arte chains. And in the post-game you get to have a dual mystic arte with Jude and Milla, I believe a first for the series.

More examples of less expansive gameplay: there's not a lot of titles (compared to roughly a billion titles in Graces F), I don't remember that there were any dumb minigames, and there were no cameo battles!

### Character Breakdown

- Milla Maxwell: Really the driving force behind the plot and the rest of the party kind of just get dragged along in her wake. In [my Berseria review](/ 2020/03/tales-of-berseria/) I said that "Milla may be my favorite character design in the Tales series overall" and I think that remains true. I love the colors in her design and the way her hair is animated, but I will easily admit that her character model has an unreasonably thin waist. (A comment I also received when I had her figurine on my desk in the office back in the day!). Milla is probably tied with Berseria's Velvet as my favorite protagonist in the Tales series to date. I mostly controlled Milla for the first playthrough.
- Jude Mathis: By contrast, I find Jude kind of meh, despite him being a co-protagonist. (Which might explain why it took me 13 years to get around to playing his side!). His character design is fine, but he kinda mostly just kind of got sucked into Milla's whirlwind and decided to throw everything away for her (which is a bit understandable, look at Milla!). I mostly controlled Jude for the 2nd playthrough, but he's kind of annoying in combat because it feels like he doesn't have a lot of range?
- Alvin: (No last name apparently). I am not a fan of that huge scarf/tie/whatever it is and combined with the fact that he is largely portrayed as double-dealing backstabber for most of the game, he is probably my least favorite character in this one. And given that he has a gun, he should have more ranged attacks!
- Elize Lutus: My second favorite character design in the game, Teepo notwithstanding lol. It's actually mostly because of her hair again, I feel like it's nice and colorful and animated well. Her personality isn't bad either. I didn't use her a lot in battle though, though she was often in the party as primary healer. (I seldom use the spellcasters much.)
- Rowen J. Ilbert: He's... okay? Mandatory old man member etc. Tied for the most interesting backstory among the human party members.
- Leia Rolando: She's kind of meh, a bit like Jude, though I like her character design a bit better. Also, she's another character who doesn't feel like she has a strong reason to be in this party! I did have her in the party often as secondary healer.
