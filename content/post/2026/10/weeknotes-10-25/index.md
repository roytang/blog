### The World

The US midterms are in less than 10 days! I say this with a mixture of dread and anticipation for the world.

The cybersecurity industry has had quite a week:

- [Eight intermediate certificate authorities were compromised](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/the-internet-goes-on-high-alert-after-discovery-of-8-compromised-tls-certs/) because of a massively bad practice:

    > The compromised eMudhra keys were defective at a level that’s challenging the imagination of many of the engineers studying the incident. A normal size for a NIST P-384 elliptic curve cryptography private key contains roughly 384 bits of entropy, a size allowing almost 2384, or a number with 116 digits, different possibilities. The private key sizes of the compromised keys ranged from 3 to 8 bits. Astonishingly, the private keys were 4, 56, 66, 115, 155, 156, 230, 237.

    Cue the many references to XKCD #221. This was a fail so bad, the provider just straight up [quit the industry](https://www.computerworld.com/article/4239619/emudhra-quits-global-tls-certificate-market-after-compromise-of-ca-signing-keys.html).

- Almost immediately after that incident came out, two new critical PHP vulnerabilities were discovered that affected something like 70% of sites on the internet, forcing many systems to be temporarily taken offline for emergency patching.

Legendary PC RPG maker Bioware has been shut down by EA. Unsurprising after the recent Saudi Arabia EA buyout, but still a sad moment considering Bioware's legacy. I played a lot of their games from the olden days, though during the past decade or so they haven't actually been very good.
