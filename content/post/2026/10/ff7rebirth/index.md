I finished the first game in this trilogy [back in 2020](/2020/07/ff7r-review/) and put off the 2nd game because I didn't have a PS5. This year a friend lent me his PS5 for a few months andthere were only 2 games I wanted to play on the system: Marvel's Spider-Man 2 ([already done](/2026/03/spiderman2-ps5/)) and *Final Fantasy VII Rebirth*. Both of these are also available on Steam, but with a PS5 I was able to borrow physical copies and essentially play them for free! Spider-Man went by quick, but Rebirth took forever. In summary, it's a beautiful open world game expanding the story of FF7 with way too much stuff to do.

The story in Rebirth picks up right after the departure from Midgar and covers until the events in the Temple of the Ancients. If you played the original, you know that's the end of Disc 1, which means the final game (coming next year) will have a lot of ground to cover, Disc 2 and Disc 3.

Same as in *Remake*, the story is greatly expanded. The game is divided into 14 chapters taking place over 7 open-world regions. It still *mostly* follows the plot of the original, yet somehow manages to intersperse visions of an alternate timeline (?). There are some areas here like Gongaga that I don't remember at all from the original, but I was happy to be reminded. One thing I don't like is how they altered the presentation of a key sequence towards the end at the Temple of the Ancients. I understand this may be to create some ambiguity as to whether things unfolded the same way, but I just have so many ideas as to how much better it could have been done.

Red XIII becomes a playable character after we rescued him in *Remake*. Yuffie also joins the party; she was playable in the *Intergrade* version of *Remake*, but I never played that so this is my first time with her. Cait Sith also joins us at the Saucer as expected, and he's the oddest duck of the cast (unsurprising). We also meet Cid and Vincent along the way, but they are not yet playable in this game.

The action-based combat remains impressive and has been iterated upon and improved after Remake. This feels like the peak Kingdom Hearts-style combat and I think it's certainly a lot better than the direction they tried going with in [FF15](/2017/03/review-final-fantasy-xv/) and [FF16](/2025/09/final-fantasy-xvi/). You still only get 3 playable characters at the time, though any nonactive members are running around in the background pretending to be helpful. Hot-swapping and combo attacks make things interesting.

My main complaint about the game is there is waaay too much stuff to do. And a good chunk of it is dumb minigames! Notably, I am very bad at things like that piano minigame (I was never able to score higher than a C on any track), and I am still not sure I am capable of beating that one chocobo racing track needed to complete a quest (but this one *might* be possible with a lot of practice). There was also this weird *mandatory* box-tossing minigame with Cait Sith that was super frustrating to get through, but I got past it eventually. (A friend complains that she stopped playing the game more than a year ago because of this minigame!)

I did enjoy the *Queen's Blood* minigame and I'm glad it's coming back in the next game, though the ruleset seems super contrived and the UI could use a lot of work (a lot of the text is so tiny!).

On top of all the quests and minigames, there's a ton of Chadley combat simulations to beat, and hard mode is unlocked once you beat the main story. I only managed to make it partway through the Brutal combat simulations and only beaten 1 chapter on hard mode so far.

### Playtime and Completion

All of this wouldn't be a problem if I owned the console and the game I was playing with, then I could just take my sweet time and ping away at the multitude of stuff to do, whittling it away slowly. And even then I might not be able to complete it, given stuff like the piano minigame mentioned above. My playtime already came in at around 110 hours. [HLTB](https://howlongtobeat.com/game/109434) says median completionist time for this game is 161 hours!! For comparison, my completionist time for *Remake* was at just under a hundred hours.

As of now, my trophy commpletion stands at 49 out of 61, tracked via [psnprofiles](https://psnprofiles.com/trophies/26098-final-fantasy-vii-rebirth/legendaryroy):

Missing achievements include: beat all of the combat sims, beat all of the hard difficulty chapters, beat all chocobo races, complete all quests (the chocobo racing one is the only I am missing), get A ranks in all the piano songs (!!!), get an S rank in the Loveless play (this one will take many many tries).

I had to return the borrowed PS5 early last month so my progress has stopped there. I may have a chance to borrow it again soon, but IDK if I have 50 more hours of this game in me so I'm calling it for this game for now. I may still plink away at some of the simulations and hard mode chapters now and again so I may still get those trophies, but I feel it is unlikely I am completing this game.

### Revelations

Despite my complaints I did enjoy the game and am looking forward to the third game coming out next year. I am inclined to get the game on Steam this time, possibly in a bundle with Remake Intergrade and this game to give me the option of replaying them on PC. Hopefully the performance isn't terrible! In fact, it would be great if it could be played on Steam Deck, though that suggested 200gb install will be an issue.
