This is my set recap for the **Hobbit** MTG set! I honestly kind of forgot about the set, so this is coming a bit late.

I knew going on that I would not be playing a lot of this set. It was the third "small set" in recent times, and the first two ([Spider-Man](/2025/11/mtg-spiderman-om1/) and [TMNT](/2026/05/mtg-tmnt/)) were not very good limited environments. The smaller set size leads to samey drafts which is boring. So for that reason and the fact that I was looking forward to the next set **Reality Fracture**, I decided ahead of time to not do too many drafts of this set and save resources for the next one.

<!--more-->

### SOS Limited Summary / Notes

- I played a mere 12 drafts for this set, which is probably less than half of the amount I play for typical sets. I only played during the first quick draft period. Coincidentally I did not play a lot of [Lord of the Rings](/2023/08/mtgltr-limited-recap/) either.
- My win rate was about average.
- HOB was *not* a well-balanced limited format. Rakdos was way too strong compared to the other archetypes, and for that reason it was my most played archetype. 
- Despite that, WR was my winningest archetype, though that may be due to the small sample size and the fact that one of my two trophies for this set was in WR.
- The imbalance felt largely due to the abundance of strong removal in red and black, exacerbated by the small set sameyness.
- The blue decks were generally terrible, with not a lot of good payoffs, and a lot of things had to come together for them to get anywhere.

### Mechanics

Each of the factions in this set has a new or returning mechanic:

- Recruit (Humans): This was an ok mechanic, feels like a variant of connive tailored to support some of the set mechanics.
- Storied (Dwarves): A variant of historic and ascend. This was fine, but I wish "enduring story" had a better term.
- Amass (Goblins): Returning mechanic, this time with a goblin variant.
- Landfall (Elves): Returning mechanic, kind of boring.
- Ferocious (Wolves): Returning mechanic, kind of boring.

### Flavor

- I am a big Marvel fan of course, and I was happy with a lot of the implementations and deep cuts here. The fact that B-list super heroes like {{< card >}}Justice, Vance Astrovik{{< /card >}} and Hazmat ({{< card >}}Hero in Training{{< /card >}}) were limited format all-stars is great.
- I had one game where Galactus was coming and I couldn't do anything about it. I lost but it felt like an amazing flavor win.
- Favorite card design: {{< card >}}Speedball{{< /card >}}. A unique and innovative protection ability that actually fits in red and conveys Speedball's powers flavorfully.
