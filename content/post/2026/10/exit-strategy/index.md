★★★★

Nothing like a short book to get you out of a reading slump. This is only the third [book I've read this year](/lists/media/books/#2026)! A friend lent me her copy last month and I just now got around to reading it. A quick read like the others, took three sittings.

Book 4 closes the arc that started in [All Systems Red](/2025/09/all-systems-red/) and sees Murderbot dealing with the consequences of his actions from all the way back to the beginning and coming to terms with his existence as a rogue SecUnit. Still a fun quick read and this time the stakes are higher with more meatbag humans to protect, people he actually knows this time, so he does his thing a bit less begrudingly.

> So the plan wasn't a clusterfuck, it was just circling the clusterfuck target zone, getting reading to come in for a landing.

Such elegant sentences lol.

This is overall an interesting universe, and the arc's ending leaves more to explore and more questions about where Murderbot goes next. I'm actually not sure why the first season of the HBO adaptation covers the entire first book, that seems like not a lot for an entire TV season. I feel like the whole 4-book arc could be covered in a single season of modern prestige TV, but what do I know? The friend who lent me these books is insisting I watch the show already, but who knows when I actually get around to it.

She also lent me book 5 (*Network Effect*), which is a much thicker book and supposedly a standalone story, so I'll see if I can get around to reading that soon.
