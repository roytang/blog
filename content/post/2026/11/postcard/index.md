I've never received a postcard.[^1] It's not really a thing people do over here. 

So when I saw [this service](https://nantucketlit.com/postcards.php) that sends you out a free sample postcard, I thought "OK neat, I'll try that". They promised not to sell my data and it looks nothing like a big corporate thing. They apparently sell subscriptions with these postcards. Anyway, I signed up, and a guy emailed me to confirm the details and he sent it out to me last month.

The postcard arrived this morning all the way from Denver, Colorado, a little bit worse for the wear:

{{< photos >}}

TBH, I did not have a lot of faith in our local postal system so I would not have been surprised if it did not arrive at all. But it did! It was a pleasant surprise. Based on the stamped dates, I would guess it arrived at our local post office exactly a month after it was sent, and got to our house a few days later. This postcard was for October, so the short story on it is Halloween-themed.

[^1]: Not via mail anyway. I think someone brought me a postcard from vacation at some point.
