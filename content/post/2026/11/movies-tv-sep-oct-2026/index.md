### Movies

Not a lot of movies during this period, I basically skipped all of September. I am likely to end up with a much lower total number of movies watched this year compared to the last year and I keep having to remind myself that it's fine, I'm not a corporation bound by "line must go up"!

#### A Time to Kill (1996)

Watched on 2026-10-11: ★★★



{{% photos a-time-to-kill-1996 %}}

 Decided to watch this because it was leaving Netflix soon and I hadn't seen anything John Grisham in a while. I was not expecting it to be so heavily about race relations in the American South in the 1980s. That time feels so recent yet the racism portrayed here seems unimaginable today. Anyway, the lawyer parts here were good, but the trial ended up being less about the law and more about emotion and overcoming racism. Not what I expected, but that's fine, still a decent watch.

### TV

#### ER

Watched on 2026-09-29: ★★★½

This one ran from 1994-2009. FIFTEEN SEASONS!! This one understandably took forever; I started watching it in June and it took me through most of September so it was a good 4 months, or around a week per season.

I mostly wanted to watch this after watching Noah Wyle in the Pitt, so as to contrast his older doctor role with the younger one that he became famous for. I was not disappointed; Wyle's Carter is definitely the defining character of ER for most of it's run and while he left for a few seasons, he was back for the end. He came in as a bright-eyed third-year medical student and we watched him grow and make many mistakes and learn from the more senior doctors and he ended up as an experienced resident who worked in third-world countries and funded an entire new hospital (John Carter was of course very much a nepo baby!). The rest of the cast was mostly great as well, but none lasted nowhere near as long as Carter did, and it was fun to see many of the older characters come back to cameo in the final season. Even the ones who died tragic deaths!

Another great thing about watching this series for the first time in 2026 is how much it reflects the time period it was in. At the time, HIV/AIDS was a big life-changing thing, and many of the show's earlier plot lines reflected that. Cancer back then was much more of a death sentence, though new treatments were being discovered all the time, many of them were experimental, and this was shown with the death of a major character in the first half of the series.

It's also wild to see how many now well-known actors had appearances or small guest roles on ER. Off the top of my head, in random order: Josh Randor, Michael Pena, Aaron Paul, Don Cheadle, Djimon Honsou, Lea Salonga (!!), Omar Epps (before he got rezzed in House), Ewan McGregor, Lucy Liu, etc. Linda Cardellini was a regular and a big part of the show's second half! Alex Kingston also had a big part in the early to middle seasons; I previously only knew her as River Song from Doctor Who. The early seasons also had up-and-coming actor George Clooney. The later season had surprising series regulars like Angela Basset and Stanley Tucci. I'm sure there are many more I forgot to note.

#### Matlock S1-2

Finished watching on 2026-10-31: ★★★½

At first I thought this was a straight-up gender-bent remake of the old Matlock TV series. Turns out that's what they wanted us to think. Instead, it's about a lawyer (who constantly references the old TV series) who lies about her identity to work at a law firm. We have of course seen this kind of deception-centered premise before (Suits), and typically the problem with this kind of show is that a lot of the ongoing tension centers around the overarching plot and the protagonist trying to keep her secret, and that can be hard to execute.

Thankfully, the execution was mostly alright. The week to week cases were generally good, and unlike Suits, the show did not take forever to resolve its overarching plot. The protag is discovered towards the end of season 1 in a cliffhanger which leads to a lot of mistrust and messy character drama in season 2. 

I was actually super annoyed with the show at that point and considered dropping it after the season, but they eventually found a decent ending, resolving the initial overarching plot and giving the show a fresh start moving forward, so I will probably give it another chance.
