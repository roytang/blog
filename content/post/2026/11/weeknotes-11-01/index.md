It is a new month! This weekend is [Undas](https://tl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undas) - a time when traditionally, Filipino families go to the cemeteries to visit their departed loved ones. As you can imagine, the cemeteries are very crowded on these days. Lots of people also travel home to the provinces. But for our household I think it has been quite a few years since we observed this tradition on the date.
