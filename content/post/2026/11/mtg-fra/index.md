This is my set recap for the **Reality Fracture** MTG set! 

<!--more-->

### Limited Summary / Notes

- I played a lot of Reality Fracture, a total of 35 events, the most I've played for a set since Final Fantasy last year. (Though I did also play 35 events during SOS season, except a good chunk of them were EOE events.)
- This set was very good to me, especially in Quick Draft and my one Premier Draft, with both formats giving me above-average win rates.
- However, my overall win rate was dragged down because I decided to try getting into Traditional Draft for the first time. (See below.) Even with TD dragging me down, the overall win rate still came in slightly above my running average!
- Without Trad Draft, my win rate would come in at a bit above 60%, which would have been my second-best win rate historically after [Wilds of Eldraine](/2023/10/mtgwoe-limited-recap/).
- I think the limited format was well-designed and decently balanced, though I definitely favored some colors like RG, which ended up being my most-played color pair, but only second most winningest after UW.
- Some card-specific notes:
    - For the RG creatures with {{< card >}}Soul Tether{{< /card >}} as the prepared spell, I find that the 1-drop uncommon {{< card >}}Heartwood Crafter{{< /card >}} and the 2-drop common {{< card >}}Konstrari Improviser{{< /card >}} are better than the repeatable {{< card >}}Woodwork Prodigy{{< /card >}}, mostly because they occupy better spots in the curve.
    - {{< card >}}Keeper of the Quiet Hour{{< /card >}}: A nice little "artifact with set mechanic" that I find easily slots into any deck. Fills a good spot on the curve and the Empower Jace gives you at the very least a free surveil.
    - {{< card >}}Medic's Kitesail{{< /card >}}: Another artifact that I would often slot into any deck. The baseline of giving any creature flying is already great. It becomes even better in white or green decks especially when you have lifegain payoffs.
    - {{< card >}}Craftwork Crusher{{< /card >}}: An incredible uncommon that always made it worthwhile to play red or green.

### Traditional Draft

This set was the first time I ever really got into the Traditional Draft format on Arena. I think I maybe tried it once or twice before, but the steep drop-off in prizes just really turned me off. But this time I got into it mainly for achievement purposes lol. One of the "Conqueror" achievements for Reality Fracture required a trophy in either Sealed or Traditional Draft, and while I did do a Sealed, it felt like I was much more likely to be able to luck out in Trad Draft. 

Alas, it was not to be! I played a total of 15 traditional draft events, but never got to the fabled 3-0. While it was a bit nice playing BO3 again (something I was used to from paper drafts many years ago), I have learned that Trad Draft is a much tougher environment than Quick Draft or Premier Draft! I acknowledge this is probably largely a skill issue on my part, but I also suspect that the people who play Trad Draft are just generally more "try hard" (not necessarily a bad thing) than the ones who do Quick Draft, making it much more challenging. It doesn't help that TD is unranked - you have no idea if you are paired against much strongers players, etc.

I ended up with a truly terrible win rate in Trad Draft, losing more matches than I won, and dragging down the overall win rate for the set! I suspect I will not venture too boldly into this format again, at least not until I feel I have gotten much better at the game or the limited format is really good for me! 

### Mechanics

- Heartwood tokens: My favorite mechanic in the set! I loved drafting RG!
- Empower Jace: The only actual new mechanic in the set. This was surprisingly fun and flexible and they made good use of the design space, even outside of blue. One would think you'd get tired of Jace, but surprisingly, not really!
- Prepare: Returning from SOS, one of the twists this time is that each color pair had a cycle of creatures that all had the same prepared spell! Pretty neat.

### Flavor

I'm not that big of a Vorthos but it was nice seeing all these alternate universe versions of various characters. Jace's whole echoverse plan seemed dumb though, and I cared much less for this conclusion than I did for War of the Spark.

### Achievements

As mentioned, Reality Fracture came with its own set of limited time achievements. There were 5 "Conqueror" achievements, 10 regular "Way of the Theorist" achievements related to specific cards, and 10 hidden "Something Worth Saving" achievements that are only revealed once you achieve them, each corresponding to an alternate Echoverse version of the regular achievements. They were originally supposed to be available only until Nov 10 (when the next set releases) but were extended to Feb (when Nauctis releases).

{{< photos achievements >}}

These were mostly pretty fun and I managed to get them all EXCEPT for the one that requires a trophy in either sealed or traditional draft, and that one will be forever out of reach since old sets never get sealed or trad draft again. I think I managed to get most of these via constructed, not letting them compromise my draft games!
