> Telling people to stop doing the job they signed up for in favor of babysitting LLMs so they can do that job instead is a great way to make people less smart about the system they used to work on and give less of a shit about it. Every prompt moves them one step further away from the system they used to know inside and out. 
<!--sep-->
> Every generated output that becomes part of the system means those people understand less about how the system actually works. Those people are robbed of the things that make them smart (the struggle of building and learning) and the things that made them gave a shit (a sense of ownership and close connection with the system).
