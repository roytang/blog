> You see, when I talk to a lot of people about web archiving, they always seem to believe it’s something they couldn’t do. It’s something a big organization like the Internet Archive does, and if you want a webpage to be saved you just have to wait and hope it gets done. But that’s not true! What I really want everyone to know is that you have control over the world around you, you can take action to preserve things and make things better.
