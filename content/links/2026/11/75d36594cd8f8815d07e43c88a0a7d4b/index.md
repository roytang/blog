(Sadly Instagram)

> I want a dumb phone and a smart president
> 
> I want slow fashion and fast friends
> 
> I want to be bored and benevolent, silly and still
> 
> I want innovation that doesn’t kill and news that doesn’t break
> 
> I want your email to find me lingering and lallygagging pondering and present
>
