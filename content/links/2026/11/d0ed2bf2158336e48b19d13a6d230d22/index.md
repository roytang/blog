> What I always valued about these projects most was the care, and my heart is filled with joy when I see people that still do. But it’s getting harder to parse that part out, and I grow tired of weighing the hearts of strangers against a feather. There is so much noise now that  the signal can often be barely perceived, and projects I’d normally be excited about are getting crowded out by the loud and the careless.
