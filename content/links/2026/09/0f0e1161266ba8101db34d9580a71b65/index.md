> A lot of people seem to be realizing that knowledge work is mostly pointless. AI might give us the pleasure of finding out what happens if an entire class of workers loses faith in their careers.
<!--sep-->

> This is Workism’s most convincing argument: The work must be important because, well, we’re all here, aren’t we? Signing in. Staying late. Every week.
<!--sep-->

> And once freed from the false satisfaction of believing we are changing the world with our day jobs, perhaps we will be inspired to fill that hole with something real — true altruism, not its artificial Workism substitute — by actually trying to impact real people, in our communities, in real ways.
