> Computers don’t make mistakes. Algorithms are free from bias. These are common lies which allow companies and governments to avoid responsibility. It’s a rehashing of the old favourite, “these are the rules, I can’t help you” but more insidious: it’s harder to prove malice the more complicated and opaque the system is.
> 
<!--sep-->
> Responsibility laundering is a time-honoured technique, employed by governments and corporations worldwide. It’s easier with computers although it still requires a bit of skill to pull off: you can’t just email your engineers to “make the algorithm reject these people”.

<!--sep-->
This kind of responsibility laundering can only be exacerbated by the use of GenAI.
